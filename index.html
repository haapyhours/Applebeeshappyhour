Avoid a Wasted Trip

How to Check Your Local Applebee's

There is no single national schedule that guarantees the same Happy Hour at every restaurant. Use the restaurant's location information before you leave.

Select Your Restaurant

Open the official Applebee's location finder or app and choose the restaurant you plan to visit.

Check Bar Specials

Look for Happy Hour or bar specials listed for that location. Weekend afternoon times may differ.

Call If It Is Unclear

Ask for today's Happy Hour times, eligible appetizers, drink specials, and whether the offer is bar-area only.

Frequently Asked Questions

Applebee's Happy Hour FAQ

What time does Applebee's Happy Hour start?

Many locations commonly start afternoon Happy Hour around 3 PM and run it until about 6 PM. Some restaurants may use different times.

Does Applebee's have a late-night Happy Hour?

Many locations commonly offer a late-night window beginning around 9 PM and continuing until close. Check your local restaurant because schedules vary.

Are Applebee's appetizers half price during Happy Hour?

Half-price appetizers are common at participating locations, but the eligible items and exact discount can vary by franchise.

Does Applebee's Happy Hour apply to takeout?

Happy Hour pricing is commonly designed for dine-in or the bar area. Do not assume the same discount applies to takeout or delivery.

Are Applebee's Happy Hour drink prices the same everywhere?

No. Drink promotions can vary by location and state alcohol laws. Local franchise policies also affect the available specials.

Does Applebee's Happy Hour run on Saturday and Sunday?

Late-night Happy Hour is commonly available at many locations, while weekend afternoon hours are more likely to vary.
